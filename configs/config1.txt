Definitions

affine equation:  x' = x1*x + x2*y + x3
                  y' = y1*x + y2*y + y3

variation equations: 
    linear      (x', y') = (x, y)
    sinusoidal  (x', y') = (sin x, sin y)
    spherical   (x', y') = (x, y) / r^2
    swirl       (x', y') = (x sin r^2 - y cos r^2, x cos r^2 + y sin r^2)
(note: r^2 = x^2 + y^2)
